# Avoiding Game-Bans

#### **1. Use a Good Account**

* Always use an **aged account** with real playtime.
* Avoid fresh or boosted accounts.

#### **2. Prepare Your PC**

* Do a **fresh Windows install** if you've been banned before (safest option).
* **Disable your antivirus** before launching the emulator.

#### **3. Cheat Smart**

* Stay **human-like**: No obvious aimbot, no wallhack abuse, no crazy killstreaks.
* Play legit for the first 5–10 hours on a new account.
* Don't chase insane stats (high KD, perfect accuracy, etc.).

#### **4. Protect Your IP**

* Change your IP after any ban (restart router or use a residential VPN).

#### **Golden Rule**

Most bans come from **player reports + manual review**, not just the anticheat. The less obvious you are, the longer your account survives.

### **Cheat smart = longer account life.**
